## Priority Checklist for UI/UX & Web Development:
- **3D & WebGL**: Implement responsive, memory-safe Three.js interactive scenes.
- **Micro-Animations**: Spring-based transitions, hover scales, magnetic buttons, and glass sheen.
- **Design Tokens**: Multi-palette, light/dark harmonious themes, accessible typography scales.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, full keyboard operability, ARIA attributes.
